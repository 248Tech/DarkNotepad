# Code of Conduct

We follow the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

**Our pledge:** make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, experience level, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

**Expected:** be respectful, welcome different viewpoints, give and accept constructive feedback gracefully, and focus on what is best for the community.

**Unacceptable:** harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, publishing others' private information, and other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

**Enforcement:** maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits and issues that violate this code. Report problems privately through a [GitHub security advisory](https://github.com/248Tech/DarkNotepad/security/advisories/new) or by contacting [@248Tech](https://github.com/248Tech).
